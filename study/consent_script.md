# Consent script (read aloud)

"Thanks for helping with our AI class project. MogScan is an app that looks at faces through a webcam.
It estimates an attractiveness score on the 'PSL' scale that's used online, and it explains which
measured facial proportions pushed the score up or down.

Some things to know before you agree:

- Everything runs on this laptop. **No photos or video are saved or uploaded.** We save a log of the
  numbers the app produces, like scores and frame rate, under a code like P03, not your name. If you
  upload a photo, it is only opened in this browser tab; it is not saved or sent anywhere.
- The score comes from a model trained on one public dataset, where 60 people rated faces. That dataset has
  its own biases. The score isn't a fact about you, and it definitely isn't a measure of your worth.
- Some people find being rated uncomfortable. You can skip the scan, skip any survey question, or stop at
  any time. You don't have to give a reason.
- You need to be 18 or older to take part.

Do you agree to take part?"

Record a verbal "yes" by checking the consent box on the first question of the form.
